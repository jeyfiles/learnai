Contrast checks (WCAG AA): body ink 14.2:1, muted text 5.5:1, white on the sage 700 button 6.7:1, input borders 3.5:1. White on the logo sage is 3.5:1. That is fine for the logo and for large headings on the green band, but too low for small text, so buttons and links use the darker sage 700.
