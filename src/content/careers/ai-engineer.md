## How to start

1. Finish Level 1, including the builds "Understand a short piece of AI code" and "Compare two versions of your own prompt".
2. Read the official API documentation of one AI provider and build a tiny app that calls a model.
3. Add an evaluation to it: ten test inputs, clear criteria and scores. Write it up.

## What to watch for

This path moves fast. Official documentation and release notes are more reliable than tutorials, which go out of date quickly.
