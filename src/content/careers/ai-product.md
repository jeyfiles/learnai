## How to start

1. Finish Level 1, especially "Test two AI tools on the same task" and the model card workbook.
2. Pick a product you use and write a one-page brief for one AI feature it could add, including how you would test it.
3. Practise explaining AI limits in plain words. It is one of the most valued skills in this path.

## What to watch for

Entry-level product roles are competitive. Experience in a field, such as healthcare or finance, plus strong AI judgement is often a good way in.
