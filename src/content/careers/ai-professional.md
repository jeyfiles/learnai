## How to start

1. Finish Level 1, especially "Use AI responsibly at work" and "Test two AI tools on the same task".
2. Pick one repeat task from your field and practise it with AI every week.
3. Write it up as a short case study and add it to your portfolio page.

## What to watch for

Job titles for this path vary a lot. Search for your usual job title plus words like "AI" or "generative AI", and read what the postings ask for.
