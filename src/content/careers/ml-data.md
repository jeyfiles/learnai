## How to start

1. Finish Level 1, especially the study mode lesson, and learn two or three core concepts properly.
2. Take one free, well-known course in machine learning basics alongside Level 2.
3. Write up one small analysis or evaluation, with the method and its limits stated clearly.

## What to watch for

Some roles on this path, especially research roles, often ask for a related degree. Check real postings in your country before you plan.
