---
term: Academic integrity
category: School and honesty
definition: Doing your own work honestly and giving credit to the ideas, words and help you used.
whyItMatters: Using AI in a way your school does not allow can count as cheating, even if you did not mean it to.
example: Using AI to quiz yourself is fine at most schools. Handing in an essay the AI wrote usually is not.
