---
term: AI agent
category: Building with AI
definition: An AI system that can take steps on its own to reach a goal, such as searching, filling in forms or using other apps.
whyItMatters: Because an agent can act, you need clear limits and a person who checks before anything important is sent or saved.
example: An agent that finds internship deadlines and adds them to your calendar, after you approve each one.
related:
- automation
