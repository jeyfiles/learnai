---
term: AI use note
category: Responsible use
definition: A short, honest statement that says which AI tool you used, what you asked it to do and what you changed or checked yourself.
whyItMatters: Employers, clients and courses increasingly ask for one. It shows you used AI openly and checked the result yourself.
example: '"I used Gemini to draft a first summary of the report. I checked every number against the original and rewrote the conclusion myself."'
