---
term: Artificial intelligence (AI)
category: Basics
definition: Computer systems that do tasks we usually link with human thinking, such as understanding language, spotting patterns or making predictions.
whyItMatters: AI is a broad word. Knowing which kind of AI a tool uses helps you guess what it is good at and where it fails.
example: A spam filter, a map app that predicts traffic and a chat tool are all forms of AI.
