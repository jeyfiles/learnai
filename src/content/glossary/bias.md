---
term: Bias
category: Checking answers
definition: A pattern in an AI's answers that unfairly favours or leaves out certain people, places or ideas, often because of its training data.
whyItMatters: Bias can show up in examples, images and advice. Asking for other viewpoints or checking other sources helps you spot it.
example: Asked for "a picture of an engineer", a tool keeps showing only one kind of person.
related:
- training-data
---
