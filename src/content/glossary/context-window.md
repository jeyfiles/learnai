---
term: Context window
category: Prompting
definition: The amount of text a model can take into account at one time, including your messages, files and its own replies.
whyItMatters: In a very long chat, early details can drop out or get less attention. For a big task, start a fresh chat with only what matters.
example: After a long chat, the tool forgets a rule you gave at the start. Repeat it or start a new chat.
related:
- token
---
