---
term: Context
category: Prompting
definition: 'The background information the tool has while answering: your earlier messages, any files you added and any instructions you gave.'
whyItMatters: Good context gives answers that fit your situation. Old or unrelated context can pull the answer off track.
example: Telling the tool your background, your target role and the job posting you are looking at is giving it context.
related:
