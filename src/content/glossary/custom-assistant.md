---
term: Custom assistant
category: Tools and features
definition: A saved set of instructions that makes a chat tool act the same way every time, such as GPTs in ChatGPT or Gems in Gemini.
whyItMatters: It saves you typing the same setup again and keeps answers consistent.
example: An "interview coach" assistant that always asks you one question at a time and gives feedback on your answer.
related:
- prompt
