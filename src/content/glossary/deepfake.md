---
term: Deepfake
category: Responsible use
definition: A fake image, video or audio clip made with AI to look or sound like a real person.
whyItMatters: Deepfakes can spread false stories and hurt people. Making one of a real person without permission can be harmful and, in many places, illegal.
example: A video that seems to show a famous person saying something they never said.

- personal-data
