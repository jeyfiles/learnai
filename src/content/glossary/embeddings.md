---
term: Embeddings
category: Building with AI
definition: Lists of numbers that represent the meaning of text, images or other data, so that similar things end up with similar numbers.
whyItMatters: Embeddings let software find things by meaning rather than exact words. They power search, recommendations and RAG.
example: A search for "refund rules" finds a page titled "Returning an item" because their embeddings are close.
