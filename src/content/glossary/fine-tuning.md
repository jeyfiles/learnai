---
term: Fine-tuning
category: Building with AI
definition: Training an existing model a little further on your own examples, so it gets better at a specific task or style.
whyItMatters: It can improve results for narrow tasks, but it costs time and data. Often a clear prompt or RAG solves the problem first.
example: Fine-tuning a model on thousands of labelled support tickets so it sorts new tickets more accurately.
related:
- training-data
- evaluation
