---
term: Grounding
category: Checking answers
definition: Making a tool base its answer on specific sources you choose, instead of on everything it learned.
whyItMatters: Grounded answers are easier to check and stay close to the material you trust. The same idea powers many AI products built on company documents.
example: Gemini Notebook answers from the files you upload and shows which file each point came from.
