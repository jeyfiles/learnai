---
term: Hallucination
category: Checking answers
definition: When an AI tool states something false as if it were true, such as a made-up fact, quote, statistic or source.
whyItMatters: Hallucinations can look very convincing. Check names, numbers, dates, quotes and references before you use them.
example: A tool lists a book as a source, but when you search for it, the book does not exist.
related:
- citation
- grounding
---
