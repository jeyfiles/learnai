---
term: Model card
category: Checking answers
definition: A document published with an AI model that describes what it is for, how it was tested, and its known limits and risks.
whyItMatters: Reading model cards is how you learn what a model can really do, beyond marketing claims.
example: A model card lists the benchmarks a model was tested on and warns that it is less accurate in some languages.
related:
- evaluation
- bias
---
