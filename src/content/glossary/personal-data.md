---
term: Personal data
category: Responsible use
definition: Any information that can identify a person, such as a name, address, phone number, photo, employer or ID number.
whyItMatters: What you type into an AI tool may be stored by the company. Leave out personal details about yourself and never share other people's.
example: Remove your name and your clients' names from a report before asking an AI tool for feedback.
