---
term: Primary source
category: Checking answers
definition: The original place a fact comes from, such as a research paper, an official report, a law or a first-hand record.
whyItMatters: Summaries and news articles can change or lose details. Going to the original is the most reliable check.
example: The census report is the primary source. A news story about the census is not.
related:
- citation
---
