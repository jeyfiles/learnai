---
term: Prompt
category: Prompting
definition: 'The message you give an AI tool: your question, your instructions and any material you want it to use.'
whyItMatters: A clear prompt is the easiest way to get a better answer. Say who you are, what you need, what to use and what a good answer looks like.
example: '"I am a commerce graduate moving into AI product work. Using only the job posting below, list the must-have skills."'
