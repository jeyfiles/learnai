term: Study mode and Guided Learning
category: Tools and features
definition: Learning features in ChatGPT (Study mode) and Gemini (Guided Learning) that ask you questions and give hints instead of just giving answers.
whyItMatters: They are built to help you understand, not just to hand over an answer. That makes them useful for learning new AI concepts properly.
example: In study mode, you ask what overfitting is and the tool first asks what you already know about training a model.
