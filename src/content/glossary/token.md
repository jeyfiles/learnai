---
term: Token
category: Prompting
definition: A small piece of text, often part of a word, that a model reads and writes. Limits on length and usage are often counted in tokens.
whyItMatters: Long files and long answers use more tokens, which can hit daily limits on free plans.
example: '"Photosynthesis" might be split into three or four tokens.'
related:
- context-window
---
