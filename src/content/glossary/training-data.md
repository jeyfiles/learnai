---
term: Training data
category: Basics
definition: The text, images or other material an AI model learned from before you ever used it.
whyItMatters: The model knows only what was in its training data, plus anything it can search or you give it. Gaps and old information in that data show up in its answers.
example: A model trained before a rule changed may describe the old rule unless it searches the web.
related:
- bias
---
