---
term: Vector database
category: Building with AI
definition: A database built to store embeddings and quickly find the ones closest in meaning to a query.
whyItMatters: Most RAG systems use one to find the right passages in large sets of documents.
example: Storing embeddings of 10,000 support articles so an assistant can find the three most relevant in a fraction of a second.
