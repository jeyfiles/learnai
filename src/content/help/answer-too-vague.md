title: The answer is too long or too general
order: 21
category: Using the tools
question: I got a wall of text that does not really fit my task.
shortAnswer: Add real details and a size limit. Tell it who you are, what the answer is for and how long it should be.
steps:
- Add who you are, for example "Class 10 student" or "first-year civil engineering".
- Say what the answer is for, for example "revision for a test on Friday".
- Set a size, for example "under 150 words" or "five bullet points".
- Give one example of the style you want.
- Ask a follow-up to trim or focus the answer.
success: The answer fits your task and is short enough to use.
prompt: Rewrite your answer for a [who you are] preparing for [purpose]. Keep it under [number] words, in [format]. Focus only on [the part you need].
