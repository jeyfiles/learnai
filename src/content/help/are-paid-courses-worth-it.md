---
title: Should I pay for an AI course?
order: 4
category: Careers in AI
question: There are so many paid AI courses and bootcamps. Do I need one?
shortAnswer: Not to start. Official documentation, free university courses and free tool tutorials cover a lot. A paid course can help if it gives you structure, feedback or projects you cannot get elsewhere. Be careful with promises of guaranteed jobs or salaries.
steps:
- Finish Level 1 here first, for free, so you know which path you want.
- Before paying, check the course syllabus against five real job postings.
- Look for real projects and feedback from people, not just videos.
- Search for independent reviews, not only testimonials on the course site.
- Walk away from any course that guarantees a job or a salary.
success: You only pay for something that fills a real gap you can name.
