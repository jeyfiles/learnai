- Read the "Good to know" note near the top of the lesson. It lists things that may look different.
- Hover over or long-press buttons to see their names.
- Look for a + button, a Tools menu or a settings menu near the message box.
- Open the official help link at the bottom of the lesson.
- If a feature is missing on your plan or in your country, use the plain chat and the same prompt.
