title: Do I need a degree in AI or computer science?
order: 3
category: Careers in AI
question: I do not have a computer science degree. Is that a problem?
shortAnswer: For many AI-related roles, no. Employers often care more about what you can show. Research and some machine learning roles are more likely to ask for a related degree. Check real postings for your target role and country.
steps:
- Collect five real postings for your target role in your country.
- Note how many require a specific degree and how many say "or equivalent experience".
- If most require a degree, look at part-time or online options alongside building a portfolio.
- If most do not, put your effort into portfolio projects that match the postings.
success: You know what your target role asks for, from real postings, not from course adverts.
