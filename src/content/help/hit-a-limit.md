shortAnswer: Free plans limit some features, like file uploads or deeper searches. Limits usually reset after some hours or the next day.
steps:
- Save your prompt and notes so you do not lose your work.
- Try again later, or the next day.
- Paste text instead of uploading a file, if the text is short.
- Use another free tool from the lesson with the same prompt.
- Plan upload-heavy tasks for earlier in the day.
