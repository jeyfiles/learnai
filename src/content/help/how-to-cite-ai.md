title: How do I say I used AI?
order: 10
category: Responsible use
question: Should I mention AI in my work, my portfolio or a course assignment? What do I write?
shortAnswer: Yes, when AI shaped the result. Add a short note that names the tool, what it did and what you checked or changed yourself. If a course or publisher uses a citation style like APA, follow its rules for AI.
steps:
- Write down the tool and the date.
- Write one sentence on what you asked the tool to do.
- Write one or two sentences on what you kept, changed and checked yourself.
- If a course, employer or publisher has a rule or style guide, follow it exactly.
- Keep the note with the work, such as at the end of a report or case study.
success: Your note is honest, short and matches what you really did.
prompt: 'Help me write a short, honest AI use note. Tool: [name]. Date: [date]. What it did: [one sentence]. What I changed and checked myself: [list]. Keep it under 70 words.'
