---
title: How do I get experience without an AI job?
order: 5
category: Careers in AI
question: Every posting wants experience. How do I get it before my first AI role?
shortAnswer: Build small, real projects and write them up. Use AI on real problems in your current work or studies, test the results, and show what you did. Volunteering and open-source projects also count.
steps:
- Pick one real problem you or people you know have.
- Solve a small part of it with AI, using what you learned in the lessons.
- Test the result with the methods from the lessons, such as scoring outputs.
- 'Write a short case study: problem, what you did, how you checked it, what you learned.'
- Add it to your portfolio and share it.
success: You have at least one real project you can talk about in an interview.
