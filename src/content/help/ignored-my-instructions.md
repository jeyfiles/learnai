title: It ignored part of my instructions
order: 14
category: When answers go wrong
question: I asked for five questions and got ten, and it gave answers when I said not to.
shortAnswer: Put your must-follow rules in a short numbered list, keep the prompt focused, and ask the tool to check its answer against your rules before it replies.
steps:
- Write your goal in one sentence.
- List your rules as numbers, for example "1. Exactly five questions. 2. No answers."
- Remove anything from the prompt that is not needed.
- 'Add: "Before you reply, check your answer against my numbered rules."'
- If the chat is long, start a new one with the clean prompt.
success: The answer follows every numbered rule.
prompt: 'Goal: [one sentence]. Rules: 1. [rule] 2. [rule] 3. [rule]. Before you reply, check your answer against each numbered rule and fix anything that breaks one.'
