title: Am I allowed to use AI for homework?
order: 1
category: School and honesty
question: My friends use AI for homework. How do I know what is allowed for me?
shortAnswer: It depends on your school, your teacher and the task. The same tool can be fine for revision and not allowed for a graded essay. When in doubt, ask before you use it.
steps:
- Look for AI rules in your student handbook, course page or class group.
- Check the assignment brief. Some tasks say "no AI" or "AI allowed for planning only".
- If nothing is written, ask your teacher a specific question, such as "Can I use AI to quiz myself before the test?"
- Write down the answer so you remember it for next time.
- If you still use AI, keep a note of how you used it.
success: You know the rule for this task, in writing or from your teacher, before you open an AI tool.
