---
title: My teacher thinks AI wrote my work. What do I do?
order: 3
category: School and honesty
question: I wrote my essay myself, but it was flagged as AI-written. How do I show it is mine?
shortAnswer: Stay calm and be honest. AI detection tools can be wrong, and your drafts and notes are the best evidence of your own work.
steps:
- Ask politely what the concern is and how it was found.
- Show your drafts, notes, outline or document version history.
- Offer to explain your work or answer questions about it in person.
- If you used AI in any way, say exactly how.
- For future work, keep your drafts and notes, and write an AI use note when you use AI.
success: Your teacher has seen how you made the work, and you know how to keep a record next time.
