title: I am under 18. What should I know first?
order: 5
category: Privacy and safety
question: Are there age rules, and do I need my parents' permission?
shortAnswer: Most AI tools need you to be at least 13, and some ask for a parent's permission until you are 18. Claude is only for adults. Each lesson tells you the age rule for its tool.
steps:
- Check the age note at the top of the lesson before you sign up.
- Talk to a parent or guardian before creating an account, especially if the tool asks for their permission.
- Use a school account if your school provides one. It may have extra protections.
- Never share personal details like your address, phone number, school name or photos of yourself.
- If something in a chat makes you uncomfortable, stop and tell a trusted adult.
success: You know the age rule for the tool, and a parent or guardian knows you are using it.
