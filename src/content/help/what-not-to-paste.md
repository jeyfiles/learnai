title: What should I never paste into an AI tool?
order: 11
category: Responsible use
question: Is it safe to paste work documents, code or data into an AI tool?
shortAnswer: Public material and your own notes are usually fine. Leave out passwords and keys, other people's personal data, and anything your employer or client has not cleared for AI tools.
steps:
- Never paste passwords, API keys, bank details or one-time codes.
- Remove names, contact details and ID numbers of real people.
- Do not paste confidential work, client files or unreleased code unless your employer allows that tool.
- Check your tool's data settings, including whether chats are used to improve the model.
- When in doubt, replace real details with made-up ones.
success: What you paste would not cause a problem if your employer or the people in it read the chat.
