question: I do not want to pay. Can I still do the lessons?
shortAnswer: Yes. Every Level 1 lesson works on a free plan. Free plans have limits, such as a few file uploads a day, and those limits change from time to time.
steps:
- Check the free plan note at the top of each lesson.
- Create free accounts with the tools you need.
- Do tasks that use uploads early in the day, since limits often reset daily.
- If you hit a limit, try the same prompt in another free tool from the lesson list.
success: You can finish the lesson without paying.
