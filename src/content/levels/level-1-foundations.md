## What Level 1 is about

Level 1 builds the habits every AI path needs. You will use common tools on small, real tasks: learning a new concept, checking a claim about a model, comparing two tools and writing up what you found. The tools will change. The habits will not.

## How an AI model works, in plain words

A language model writes an answer by predicting which words should come next, based on patterns it learned from a huge amount of text and on what you gave it. That is why it can explain a topic in seconds. It is also why it can invent a fact, a quote or a source and still sound confident. Sounding sure is not the same as being right.

## The three habits

1. **Ask clearly.** Say who you are, what you need, what to use and what the answer should look like.
2. **Test before you trust.** Check names, numbers and claims against official sources, and score outputs against criteria you set in advance.
3. **Show your work.** Save proof of what you did and how you checked it. That proof becomes your portfolio.
