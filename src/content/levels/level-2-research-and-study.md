## What Level 2 will cover

Level 2 goes deeper into learning fast and accurately. You will work on longer pieces: a research brief, a paper explainer and a study notebook for a topic you need for your target role.

## Why this level matters

AI changes every month. The people who do well are not the ones who know every tool, but the ones who can learn the next one quickly from reliable sources.
