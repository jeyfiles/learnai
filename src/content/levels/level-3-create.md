## What Level 3 will cover

Level 3 is about making things with AI: visuals for a product idea, a short explainer video for your portfolio, or an audio summary of your research. You will plan first, then create, then check.

## Why this level matters

AI media can look finished even when it contains errors, like wrong labels on a diagram or made-up text on a sign. Planning and checking are what make your work usable in a real job.
