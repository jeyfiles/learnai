## What Level 4 will cover

Level 4 moves from asking questions to building helpers. You might build a research assistant that always cites official sources, or an automation that collects job postings into one list and flags the best matches.

## Why this level matters

Tools that act on your behalf save time, and they can also repeat a mistake many times before anyone notices. This level teaches you to set limits, test safely and keep a person in the loop. Employers look for exactly this judgement.
