## What Level 5 will cover

Level 5 is about building something real and shipping it: a small AI tool for a real problem, from plan to working version to write-up.

## Why this level matters

AI can write code quickly, but the person who owns the project still decides what to build, tests it and fixes it. A shipped project with a clear write-up is one of the strongest things you can show an employer.
