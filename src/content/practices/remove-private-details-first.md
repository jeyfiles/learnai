---
title: Remove private details before you paste
order: 3
minutes: 10
skill: Responsible use
stage: worked
paths: [all]
recallQuestion: When you checked sources yesterday, what did you do when a number could not be found?
workedExample:
  situation: Lena works in HR and wants to use AI to tidy up an email to a job candidate. The draft has the candidate's full name, phone number and salary.
  input: "Dear Sam Patel, following your interview on Monday, we would like to offer you the role at 48,000 a year. Please call me on 07700 900123."
  approach:
    - She listed every detail that could identify a person or that the company would not want shared. Name, phone number, salary.
    - She swapped each one for a label in square brackets. [Candidate name], [phone], [salary].
    - She pasted only the cleaned version and asked for a warmer, clearer email.
    - She put the real details back in herself before sending.
  output: A friendlier email with the labels still in place, ready for her to fill in. No private data ever left her computer.
  whyItWorks: Most AI tools may keep what you paste, and some use it to improve their models. Labels keep the meaning of the text, so the tool can still help, without sharing anything private.
task:
  outcome: One real piece of text from your work or study, cleaned of private details and improved with AI.
  steps:
    - Pick a short email, note or message you want to improve.
    - Highlight every name, number, address, amount or company detail that should stay private.
    - Replace each one with a label in square brackets.
    - Paste the cleaned text into an AI tool with the prompt below.
    - Put the real details back in yourself.
  prompt: "Improve this message so it is clear and polite. Keep every label in square brackets exactly as it is. Here is the message: [paste cleaned text]"
successChecks:
  - Nothing you pasted could identify a real person, client or company.
  - Every label came back unchanged in the answer.
  - You would be happy for a stranger to read exactly what you pasted.
ifStuck: Ask yourself, would I post this line on a public noticeboard? If not, turn it into a label.
connectedLesson: use-ai-responsibly-at-work
