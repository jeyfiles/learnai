---
title: Build and test an AI routine for one task in your field
level: 1
path: ai-professional
order: 1
promise: Turn one task you do every week into a tested AI routine, with a prompt, a checklist and clear rules on when not to use it.
outcome: A one-page routine that a colleague could follow, tested on three real examples, plus a short case study for your portfolio.
scenario: You do the same writing or analysis task every week, such as a status update, a meeting summary or a client reply. You want AI to help, but only in a way you can trust and explain to your manager.
tools: [ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude]
minutes: 120
beforeYouStart:
  - Pick one task you do at least once a week.
  - Collect three past examples of the task. Remove names, numbers and anything private first.
  - Check your workplace rules on AI tools. If there are none, ask your manager before you use real work material.
steps:
  - title: Describe the task
    action: Write three lines. What the task is, who reads the result, and what a good result looks like to that reader.
    checkpoint: A colleague could read your three lines and understand the task without asking you anything.
  - title: Write your scoring rules
    action: Write four rules a good result must pass, each one checkable as yes or no. Include one rule about accuracy.
    checkpoint: You have four yes or no rules written before you ask any AI tool for help.
  - title: Write the first prompt
    action: Write a prompt that gives your role, the reader, the input you will paste and the format you want back.
    prompt: "I am a [role]. Each week I write [task] for [reader]. I will paste [input]. Write the result as [format], in under [length] words. Only use facts from what I paste. If something is missing, list it at the end instead of guessing."
    checkpoint: The prompt names your role, the reader, the input, the format and one rule against guessing.
  - title: Test it on three examples
    action: Run the prompt on your three cleaned examples. Score each result against your four rules.
    checkpoint: You have a table with three rows and four columns of pass or fail.
  - title: Improve one thing and retest
    action: Look at the rule that failed most. Change one thing in the prompt to fix it, then run all three examples again.
    checkpoint: At least one more cell passes than before, and you know which change caused it.
  - title: Write the routine
    action: Put the final prompt, the four rules and a "check before you send" list on one page. Add two situations where you should not use AI for this task.
    checkpoint: The page fits on one screen and includes when not to use it.
  - title: Write the case study
    action: Write three lines for your portfolio. The problem, what you built and tested, and the result.
    checkpoint: Every claim in the case study is true and you can show the score table as proof.
tests:
  - name: Rules first
    expected: Your scoring rules are dated or saved before your first AI result.
  - name: Three real examples
    expected: All three examples come from your real work, cleaned of private details.
  - name: Honest result
    expected: Your score table shows what failed as well as what passed.
safety:
  - Never paste client names, personal data or confidential numbers. Use labels in square brackets instead.
  - Follow your employer's rules on AI tools, even if they are stricter than this project.
proof:
  - The one-page routine
  - The score table from the first and second tests
  - Your three line case study
