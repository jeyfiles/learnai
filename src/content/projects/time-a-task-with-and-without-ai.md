---
title: Measure a task with and without AI
level: 1
path: ai-professional
order: 2
promise: Find out, with your own numbers, whether AI really saves you time on one task, and what it costs in checking.
outcome: A small before and after study with times, quality scores and an honest conclusion, ready to share with a manager or in an interview.
scenario: People say AI saves hours. Your manager asks whether it would help your team. You want an answer based on evidence, not on claims.
tools: [ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot]
minutes: 180
beforeYouStart:
  - Choose a task that takes between 15 and 60 minutes and that you do often.
  - Prepare six similar pieces of input, cleaned of private details.
  - Have a timer ready, such as the clock on your phone.
steps:
  - title: Choose how you will judge quality
    action: Write three quality rules for the finished task, each checkable as yes or no.
    checkpoint: You can score any result from 0 to 3 using your rules.
  - title: Do three without AI
    action: Do the task three times the way you normally do it. Time each one, including checking.
    checkpoint: You have three times and three quality scores.
  - title: Write your AI prompt
    action: Write one prompt for the task. Give your role, the reader, the input and the format.
    checkpoint: The prompt works on one test input without major fixes.
  - title: Do three with AI
    action: Do the other three with AI. Time everything, including writing the prompt, reading the answer, checking facts and fixing mistakes.
    checkpoint: You have three more times and three more quality scores, and the times include checking.
  - title: Compare
    action: Put all six rows in a table. Work out the average time and average score for each way.
    checkpoint: The table shows both averages side by side.
  - title: Write the conclusion
    action: Write four sentences. What you measured, what you found, one limit of your test, and what you would recommend.
    checkpoint: Your conclusion matches your numbers, even if AI did not save time.
tests:
  - name: Checking time counted
    expected: The AI times include reading and checking, not only the time the tool took to answer.
  - name: Same kind of input
    expected: All six inputs are similar in length and difficulty.
  - name: Honest limit
    expected: The conclusion names at least one limit, such as the small number of tries.
safety:
  - Clean every input of names, numbers and private details before you paste it.
  - Do not present six tries as proof for a whole team. Say it is a small test.
proof:
  - The table with six rows
  - Your four sentence conclusion
