---
title: Build your AI portfolio page
level: 1
order: 4
promise: Turn the proof you saved in Level 1 into a simple portfolio page that shows what you can do.
outcome: A one-page portfolio with three short case studies, ready to share with employers.
minutes: 120
paths:
- all
tools:
- ChatGPT
- Gemini
beforeYouStart:
- Collect the proof you saved from Level 1 lessons and workbooks.
- Pick where your page will live, such as a free site builder, a GitHub page or a shared document.
- Remove any private or confidential information from your examples.
versions:
- path: all
  title: Simple shared document
  brief: A clean document or slide with links. Fastest to start.
- path: ai-engineer
  title: GitHub page
  brief: A repository README or a GitHub Pages site with your code and write-ups.
parts:
- title: Choose your best three
  purpose: Show quality, not quantity.
  steps:
  - title: List your proof
    action: 'List everything you saved: plans, comparisons, explainers, write-ups.'
    checkpoint: You have a full list of your proof.
  - title: Pick three
    action: Pick three pieces that show different skills, such as testing, explaining and building.
    checkpoint: You picked three pieces that show different skills.
- title: Write three case studies
  purpose: Help a stranger see what you did and why it matters.
  steps:
  - title: Use a simple structure
    action: 'For each piece, write four short parts: the problem, what you did, how you checked it, and what you learned.'
    checkpoint: Each case study has all four parts.
  - title: Get comments, not a rewrite
    action: Ask for feedback on clarity for each case study.
    clickPath:
    - chatgpt.com
    - New chat
    prompt: Here is a short portfolio case study. Without rewriting it, tell me which part a hiring manager might not understand, and what evidence is missing.
    checkpoint: You get comments you can act on.
  - title: Add your AI use note
    action: Add a one-line note to each case study saying how you used AI.
    checkpoint: Each case study says how AI was used.
- title: Publish the page
  purpose: Make it easy to share.
  steps:
  - title: Write a short intro
    action: Write three sentences about who you are, the AI path you are aiming for and what you are learning now.
    checkpoint: You have a short, honest intro.
  - title: Publish and test the links
    action: Publish the page and open every link in a private browser window.
    checkpoint: Every link works for someone who is not signed in.
tests:
- A friend can tell what you built and how you checked it in under two minutes.
- No private or confidential information appears anywhere.
- Every link works when you are signed out.
proof:
- The link to your portfolio page
sources:

  url: https://apastyle.apa.org/blog/how-to-cite-chatgpt
